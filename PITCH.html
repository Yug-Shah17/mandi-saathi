WORKSHOP PROTOTYPE / MAHARASHTRA

Better decisions
before the mandi.

Mandi Saathi helps explore what may remain after selling costs, and what waiting would require. A focused prototype for Nashik onion market decisions.

No live feed · no prediction · no verified claim of farmer income gains

01 / THE PROBLEM

A higher price can cost more to reach.

A farmer choosing where to sell needs more than a price on a screen. Transport, commissions, crop quality and the age of the report can change the decision.

Waiting has trade-offs too: storage costs, possible spoilage and the need for cash. Future prices are uncertain.

Context: Competition Commission onion market study (historical evidence, not a claim about all farmers today).

02 / WHAT EXISTS

We are entering an existing market.

e-NAM

Mai Kisaan

Marketplace and dated mandi reports, including Marathi.

ACROP

Market reports, crop information and paid alerts.

Our hypothesis: a clear, input-based comparison of possible proceeds can complement existing price information. This is not yet proven unique or validated with farmers.

05 / BUSINESS HYPOTHESIS

Who might pay?

An FPO, cooperative or adviser may pay for a reliable tool that helps its staff support many farmers. The farmer could use the core tool without a personal subscription.

  • Test whether the organisation sees measurable value and has a budget.
  • Measure data, support, onboarding and distribution costs.
  • Do not claim profitability until willingness to pay and costs are verified.

Other possible models include transaction services or buyer analytics, but each introduces operational costs and incentive conflicts.

06 / TRUST AND NEXT STEPS

What we know. What we don't.

Built

Two-market arithmetic, local CSV import, historical example and sell-later scenario.

Not yet built

Automatic current prices, prediction, live bids, transactions or a verified forecast.

Not yet validated

Farmer usability, real-world impact, Marathi wording and customer willingness to pay.

Next: verify access to a reliable current data feed, test with farmers and FPOs, and improve the decision workflow from their feedback.
